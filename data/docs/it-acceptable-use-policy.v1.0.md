# Acceptable Use of IT Systems

1. Scope. This policy applies to every employee, contractor and temporary worker who uses Corvane IT systems, whether in the Dublin or Lisbon office, at home or while travelling. Corvane IT systems include laptops and accessories issued by Corvane, the Corvane ID account and every service you sign in to with it, email, Teams, Assist, Orbit HR, the SecureLink VPN, the office networks and any cloud service the company pays for. The policy also applies when you access Corvane data from a device Corvane does not own. By signing in with your Corvane ID you confirm that you have read this policy and will follow it.
2. Business purpose. Corvane IT systems are provided so that you can do your job and serve our customers. You are responsible for everything done with your account and your devices, so you must never share your password, your Corvane Verify approvals or your Lockbox vault with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff. The IT Service Desk will never ask for your password. If you believe someone else has used your account, report it to Security Operations straight away as described in it-security-incident-reporting.
3. Personal use. Limited personal use of Corvane systems is allowed in moderation, for example checking a news site during a break, booking a medical appointment or a short personal call on Teams. Personal use must not interfere with your work or other people's work, must not use large amounts of bandwidth or storage, and must not break any other clause of this policy. Corvane does not back up personal files, and personal files on a company laptop may be erased when the laptop is replaced, wiped or returned. Personal use is a privilege that a manager can restrict if it is abused.
4. Prohibited content and activities. You must not use Corvane systems to access, store or share material that is illegal, harassing, discriminatory, sexually explicit or violent, or to gamble, mine cryptocurrency or run a personal business. You must not try to bypass security controls, scan the network, install hacking tools or access systems and data you have not been authorised to use, even out of curiosity. Security testing is carried out only by Security Operations or by testers they have approved in writing.
5. Software and configuration. Install software only from the DeviceDock Company Portal or through an approved request in Assist, as described in it-software-catalogue. Do not disable or uninstall SecureLink, DeviceDock, endpoint protection or automatic updates, and do not change system settings that IT manages. If you have temporary admin rights, use them only for the purpose you gave in the request. Browser extensions that read page content or handle passwords need the same approval as any other application.
6. Removable storage. USB storage devices, such as memory sticks and external hard drives, are blocked on all Corvane laptops by DeviceDock. Keyboards, mice, headsets and other USB peripherals still work. If you must exchange files with a customer or partner, use the company file sharing service with an expiring link. In the rare case where a USB storage device is unavoidable, for example for a customer site without internet access, Security Operations can grant a time-limited exception for an encrypted device after a request in Assist.
7. Email and data handling. Do not forward Corvane email, documents or customer data to a personal email account or personal cloud storage, and do not set up automatic forwarding rules to external addresses; such rules are blocked and reported automatically. Send customer data only to recipients who need it and check the address before you send. Classify documents according to the data classification labels in the office suite, and store work files in company cloud storage rather than only on your laptop.
8. Working outside the office. When you work from home, a café or while travelling, connect through SecureLink when you need internal systems, lock your screen whenever you step away, and do not let family members or other people use your Corvane laptop. Avoid working on confidential material where others can read your screen. Never leave a laptop unattended in a car or public place. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so that they can be wiped remotely.
9. Communication standards. Messages sent from Corvane systems can be read as statements by the company. Be professional and respectful in email, Teams and other channels, and do not speak on behalf of Corvane in public forums or on social media unless your role allows it. Do not use Corvane systems to send chain letters, unsolicited mass email or political campaigning. Teams channels are company records and may be retained and reviewed in line with the retention policy.
10. Monitoring. To protect the company, its customers and its employees, Corvane monitors the use of its IT systems. Monitoring includes logging of sign-ins, network traffic through SecureLink and the office networks, device compliance data from DeviceDock, email security scanning and alerts from endpoint protection. Monitoring is automated and proportionate; the content of an individual's messages or files is reviewed only when there is a specific security, legal or disciplinary reason, with approval from Security Operations and People Operations. You should not expect privacy for personal activity carried out on Corvane systems.
11. Breaches. Breaking this policy can lead to removal of access, disciplinary action up to and including dismissal and, where the law has been broken, referral to the authorities. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask before you act. Reporting a mistake promptly is always treated more favourably than hiding it.
12. Questions and review. This policy is owned by Security Operations and reviewed at least once a year together with People Operations. Questions about acceptable use go to security@example.com, and general IT questions go to the IT Service Desk at servicedesk@example.com or ext. 4357. Changes to the policy are announced on the intranet and take effect from the date shown at the top of this document.
