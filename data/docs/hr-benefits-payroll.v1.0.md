# Payroll and Benefits Overview

This page answers the most common questions about pay and benefits at Corvane. It is maintained by the Payroll & Benefits team within People Operations. Your contract and local law in Ireland or Portugal take precedence where they give you more.

## Payroll

### When do I get paid?

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th of the month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or a company or public holiday, you are paid on the previous working day. For example, in December 2026 the 25th is Christmas Day, and 24 December is also a company holiday, so salaries are paid on Wednesday 23 December. Payroll & Benefits confirms the December payday by email in November each year.

### What is the payroll cut-off?

The payroll cut-off is the 10th of the month. Changes that reach Payroll & Benefits by the 10th, such as approved overtime, bank detail updates, allowances or salary changes, are included in that month's pay. Anything submitted after the 10th is included in the following month. Approved expense claims follow the same cut-off.

### Where do I find my payslip?

Payslips are published in Orbit HR at orbit.example.com under Pay, two working days before payday. Annual tax statements are published in the same place. Paper payslips are not issued.

### How do I change my bank details or tax information?

Update your bank details in Orbit HR under Personal details. For security, a change to bank details triggers a Corvane Verify prompt and a confirmation email. Tax credit certificates (Ireland) and tax withholding declarations (Portugal) are uploaded in Orbit HR under Documents.

### I think my pay is wrong. What should I do?

Raise a Payroll query in Orbit HR with the payslip month and a short description. Payroll & Benefits responds within 3 working days. If an underpayment is confirmed, the difference is paid in the next payroll run, or by a separate transfer within 5 working days if the amount is significant.

## Benefits

### Which benefits do I get?

The table below lists the standard benefits for all permanent employees. Benefits for fixed-term employees are set out in their contracts.

| Benefit | Amount (EUR) | Eligibility | How to claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pension | Company match up to 6% of base salary | From the end of probation | Enrol in Orbit HR under Benefits |
| Health insurance | Fully paid for the employee | From day 1 | Automatic enrolment; add dependants in Orbit HR |
| Commuting allowance | 60 per month | Employees who commute by public transport | Request in Orbit HR under Benefits |
| Training budget | 1,000 per year | From day 1 | Request in Orbit HR under Learning |
| Home internet allowance | 20 per month | Employees with an approved hybrid or remote arrangement | Paid through payroll automatically |

### How does the pension match work?

Corvane matches your own pension contribution up to 6% of your base salary. If you contribute 4%, Corvane adds 4%; if you contribute 8%, Corvane adds 6%. Contributions are deducted through payroll each month, and you can change your contribution rate in Orbit HR once per quarter, before the 10th of the month.

### When does health insurance start?

Health insurance starts on your first day of employment, with no waiting period. You can add a partner or children at your own cost through Orbit HR within 30 days of joining or of a life event. The insurance card is sent to your home address in your first two weeks.

### Who can claim the commuting allowance?

The commuting allowance is EUR 60 per month for employees who travel to the Dublin or Lisbon office by public transport, such as bus, train, tram or metro. Request it once in Orbit HR and confirm each January that you still commute by public transport. The allowance is paid through payroll and is subject to the usual tax rules in your country. It covers public transport only; other ways of commuting are not reimbursed.

### How do I use my training budget?

Every employee has EUR 1,000 per calendar year for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets related to their role. Agree the training with your manager, then request it in Orbit HR under Learning before you book. Talent & Learning approves requests within 5 working days. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

Questions about pay go to Payroll & Benefits through Orbit HR, or to People Operations at hr@example.com or ext. 4470, Monday to Friday 09:00–17:00.
