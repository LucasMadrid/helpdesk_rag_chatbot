# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose and scope

### Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to all Corvane employees based in the Dublin and Lisbon offices, including employees on fixed-term contracts. It explains how many days you may work away from the office, what you need to do before working from another country, and what support Corvane provides for setting up a workspace at home.

Contractors and agency staff follow the arrangements in their own agreements and should check with the manager who engaged them.

### Principles

Corvane offers hybrid working because it gives people more flexibility while keeping the collaboration that happens best in person. Remote work is an arrangement agreed with your manager, not an entitlement that overrides the needs of your team, your customers or your role.

## Hybrid working pattern

### Remote days

You may work remotely for up to 2 days per week. You can choose any days of the week, as long as you agree them with your manager and record them in your team calendar. Days do not carry over: an unused remote day in one week cannot be added to the following week.

Some teams with on-site responsibilities, such as Facilities, may have fewer remote days available. Your manager will tell you if this applies to your role.

### Core hours and availability

On remote days you are expected to be available during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time (Lisbon employees use Lisbon time). Keep your Teams status up to date and make sure you can join calls with your camera on when the meeting requires it.

The standard working week is 39 hours wherever you work. See hr-working-hours-overtime for details on working hours and overtime.

## Working from abroad

### Limits and approval

You may work from a country other than the one in your employment contract for a maximum of 10 working days per calendar year. You need your manager's approval before you travel. Ask your manager by email at least two weeks ahead and include the country, the dates and the address where you will be working.

Working abroad for longer than 10 working days per year is not permitted under this policy.

Working abroad days count towards your weekly remote days. Travelling to the destination must happen in your own time or on annual leave.

### Security while abroad

Always connect through the SecureLink VPN when working outside a Corvane office. Do not use public or hotel Wi-Fi without SecureLink connected, and never leave your laptop unattended in a public place. If your laptop is lost or stolen abroad, report it immediately. See it-security-incident-reporting.

## Home-office setup

### Allowance

Corvane pays a one-off home-office allowance of EUR 300 to help you set up a safe and comfortable workspace, for example a desk, chair or monitor. Claim the allowance as an expense in Orbit HR with your receipts within 30 days of purchase. The allowance is paid once per employee, regardless of how long you stay with Corvane.

### Equipment and security

Your Corvane laptop is your main work device. Request additional peripherals such as a docking station or headset through Assist. Personal devices must not be used to access customer data. See it-acceptable-use-policy for the rules that apply to all IT systems, at home or in the office.

## Special arrangements

### Fully remote roles

Fully remote employment is not offered under this policy for new or existing employees based in Dublin or Lisbon. Employees who were already hired as fully remote before this policy took effect keep their existing contractual arrangements.

### Exceptions

Your manager may agree to a temporary change in your pattern, for example during building works or a short-term personal situation. Such changes should be confirmed in writing and reviewed at least every three months.

Exceptions on medical grounds or as a reasonable accommodation are handled by People Operations. Contact hr@example.com and we will discuss the options with you confidentially.

Managers can ask the team to be in the office on particular days, such as for planning sessions, customer visits or team events. Give at least one week's notice when you can.

Employees on probation follow the same rules as everyone else, including the number of remote days per week. Your manager may still ask you to spend more time in the office during your first weeks so that you can meet your team and your onboarding buddy.
