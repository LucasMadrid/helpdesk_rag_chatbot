# Leaving Corvane: Offboarding Checklist

This checklist explains what needs to happen when an employee leaves Corvane, whether they resign, reach the end of a fixed-term contract or leave for another reason. Each section lists the tasks for one owner: the employee, the manager and IT. People Operations opens the leaver case in Orbit HR as soon as the last working day is confirmed, and the checklist below is tracked in that case.

If you have questions at any stage, contact People Operations at hr@example.com or ext. 4470, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00.

Your final salary, including any unused leave and approved final expenses, is paid on the next normal payday, the 25th of the month, provided the leaver case is complete before the payroll cut-off on the 10th. Otherwise it is paid on the following payday. Your final payslip is sent to the personal email address in Orbit HR.

## Employee

Before your last working day:

- Confirm your last working day with your manager and check that it is recorded correctly in Orbit HR.
- Check your remaining annual leave balance in Orbit HR. Unused leave is paid out with your final salary, so you do not have to use it all before you leave, but agree with your manager whether you will take any of it during your notice period.
- Submit any outstanding expense claims in Orbit HR. Final expenses must be submitted within 10 days of your last working day; claims received after that cannot be paid.
- Hand over your work: update documentation, reassign open tickets in Assist and share any files your team will need from your OneDrive to a team site.
- Move any personal files off your laptop. Do not copy Corvane or customer data to personal devices or personal email accounts.
- Update your personal email address and home address in Orbit HR so that your final payslip and tax documents reach you.
- Set an out-of-office message for your last day that names a colleague to contact.

After your last working day:

- Return all Corvane equipment within 5 working days of your last working day. This includes your laptop, charger, docking station, headset, access badge and any other equipment issued to you.
- Use the prepaid courier label that IT sends to your personal email address. Office-based employees can instead hand equipment in at the Service Desk.
- For how laptops are logged and wiped on return, see it-laptop-request. Note that the 30-day return period described there applies only to an old laptop being replaced, not to leavers.

## Manager

- Confirm the last working day in Orbit HR as soon as you have the resignation or end date, and at least 10 working days before it where possible.
- Agree a handover plan with the employee and make sure their responsibilities are covered after they leave.
- Approve or reject the employee's final expense claims promptly so they can be paid with the final salary.
- Review shared mailboxes, Teams channels, distribution lists and systems the employee owns, and transfer ownership before the last day.
- Raise a request in Assist if the employee's mailbox should be forwarded to you or kept for a period after they leave.
- Confirm the employee's leave balance with Payroll & Benefits if any leave was taken but not yet recorded.
- Arrange an exit conversation; People Operations offers an optional exit interview as well.
- Collect the access badge if the employee works in the office and hand it to Facilities.

## IT

- Receive the leaver notification from Orbit HR and open a linked ticket in Assist.
- Disable the employee's Corvane ID, VPN access and Lockbox vault at 18:00 local time on the last working day. Earlier deactivation happens only when requested by People Operations.
- Remove the employee from Corvane Verify and revoke any registered security keys.
- Send the prepaid courier label to the employee's personal email address on the last working day.
- Track the equipment return and remind the employee if nothing has been received after 5 working days. Report unreturned equipment to the manager and People Operations.
- On receipt, check the laptop against the asset record in DeviceDock, wipe it and update the asset record.
- Keep the mailbox and OneDrive content for the retention period set by Security Operations, then delete them.
