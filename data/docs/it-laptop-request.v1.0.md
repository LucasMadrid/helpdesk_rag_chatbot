# Laptop Request and Replacement

This page explains how to request a new laptop, how scheduled replacements work and who has to approve each type of request. It applies to all Corvane employees in Dublin, Lisbon and fully remote roles. It covers laptops only; monitors, docking stations and other peripherals are ordered through the accessories form in Assist and are not described here.

Corvane offers two laptop models. The standard model (14-inch screen, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD) is issued to most roles, including Sales, Customer Success, Finance, HR and Facilities. The engineering model (16-inch screen, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD) is issued to Engineering and to Infrastructure Engineering and Security Operations staff who run local build environments or virtual machines. Both models are enrolled in DeviceDock before they leave the warehouse, so they arrive with SecureLink, Lockbox and the standard software already installed.

Laptops are replaced on a 4-year refresh cycle, counted from the date the device was first issued to you. End-User Computing contacts you about six weeks before your refresh date. You do not need to request a refresh yourself, but you can start the request up to 3 months before your refresh date if your manager agrees.

## Requesting or replacing a laptop

Follow these steps for a new starter, a scheduled refresh, an early replacement after a fault or damage, or a request to move from the standard to the engineering model.

1. Check whether you need a new device at all. Many performance problems are fixed by a restart, a DeviceDock compliance check or a reset of your profile, so contact the IT Service Desk first if your current laptop is slow or unstable.
2. Sign in to Assist at assist.example.com with your Corvane ID and complete the Corvane Verify prompt.
3. Open the catalogue, choose Hardware, then select Laptop request.
4. Select the request type: New starter, Scheduled refresh, Early replacement or Model change.
5. Choose the model. If you request the engineering model and you are not in Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering or Security Operations, add a short business justification describing the workloads you run.
6. Enter the delivery address. Office employees normally collect from the IT bar in Dublin or Lisbon; fully remote employees receive the device by courier at their registered home address in Orbit HR. New starters always receive their laptop by courier at their home address 3 working days before their start date.
7. For an early replacement, describe the fault or damage and attach a photo if the laptop is physically damaged. Accidental damage is covered, but repeated damage is reviewed with your manager.
8. For a new starter, the hiring manager submits the request at least 10 working days before the start date and enters the new starter's personal email address so the Corvane ID activation link can be sent.
9. Submit the request. Assist creates a P4 ticket and routes it to the approvers shown in the approval matrix below.
10. Wait for approval. Approvers receive an email and an Assist notification; requests that are not approved within 3 working days are escalated to the approver's manager.
11. After approval, End-User Computing prepares and enrols the laptop. Delivery takes 5 working days from approval to collection or courier hand-over.
12. When you receive the new laptop, sign in with your Corvane ID, complete Corvane Verify enrolment if prompted, and wait for DeviceDock to show the status Compliant before you start work.
13. Move your files. Work files belong in your company cloud storage, so in most cases you only need to sign in; check that nothing important is left in local folders on the old device.
14. Return the old laptop within 30 days of receiving the new one. This 30-day grace period lets you check that everything works before you hand back the old device. Drop it at the IT bar or book a prepaid courier through the Return equipment form in Assist. If the old laptop is not returned when the grace period ends, it is locked through DeviceDock and your manager is informed.

All approvals are recorded in the Assist ticket, so you do not need to forward emails to the Service Desk. If your request is urgent because you cannot work at all, for example after a laptop is lost or stolen, raise it by phone on ext. 4357 so it can be prioritised; see it-security-incident-reporting for what to do first with a lost device.

The approval matrix shows who must approve each request type for each model, and the target delivery time after the last approval.

| Request type | Standard model approver | Engineering model approver | Delivery time (working days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| New starter | Hiring manager | Hiring manager and department head | 5 |
| Scheduled refresh (4 years) | No approval needed | Line manager | 5 |
| Early replacement (fault or damage) | Line manager | Line manager and End-User Computing lead | 5 |
| Model change (standard to engineering) | Not applicable | Department head and Finance | 5 |

Questions about this process go to the IT Service Desk at servicedesk@example.com or ext. 4357, Monday to Friday 08:00–18:00 Irish time.
