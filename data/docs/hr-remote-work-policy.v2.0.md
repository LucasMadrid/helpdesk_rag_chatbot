# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose and scope

### Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to all Corvane employees based in the Dublin and Lisbon offices, including employees on fixed-term contracts. It explains how many days you may work away from the office, what you need to do before working from another country, and what support Corvane provides for setting up a workspace at home. It replaces version 1.0 of this policy from 1 March 2026.

Contractors and agency staff follow the arrangements in their own agreements and should check with the manager who engaged them.

### Principles

Corvane offers hybrid working because it gives people more flexibility while keeping the collaboration that happens best in person. Remote work is an arrangement agreed with your manager, not an entitlement that overrides the needs of your team, your customers or your role.

## Hybrid working pattern

### Remote days

You may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are office "anchor days": everyone in Dublin and Lisbon is expected in the office on those two days, so your remote days fall on Monday, Thursday or Friday. Agree your pattern with your manager and record it in your team calendar. Days do not carry over: an unused remote day in one week cannot be added to the following week.

If a company holiday falls on an anchor day, there is no replacement anchor day that week. Some teams with on-site responsibilities, such as Facilities, may have fewer remote days available. Your manager will tell you if this applies to your role.

### Core hours and availability

On remote days you are expected to be available during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time (Lisbon employees use Lisbon time). Keep your Teams status up to date and make sure you can join calls with your camera on when the meeting requires it.

The standard working week is 39 hours wherever you work. See hr-working-hours-overtime for details on working hours and overtime.

## Working from abroad

### Limits and approval

You may work from a country other than the one in your employment contract for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year. Each request needs HR approval: submit it in Orbit HR at least 15 working days before you travel, including the country, the dates and the address where you will be working. Your manager is notified automatically and can comment on the request, but People Operations makes the decision because of tax and social security rules.

If you want to work abroad for more than 20 working days in a year, contact HR.

Working abroad days count towards your weekly remote days and do not replace anchor days. Travelling to the destination must happen in your own time or on annual leave.

### Security while abroad

Always connect through the SecureLink VPN when working outside a Corvane office. Do not use public or hotel Wi-Fi without SecureLink connected, and never leave your laptop unattended in a public place. If your laptop is lost or stolen abroad, report it immediately. See it-security-incident-reporting.

## Home-office setup

### Allowance

Corvane pays a home-office allowance of EUR 500 every 3 years to help you set up and maintain a safe and comfortable workspace, for example a desk, chair or monitor. Claim the allowance as an expense in Orbit HR with your receipts within 30 days of purchase. The 3-year period starts on the date of your first claim.

In addition, every employee on a hybrid or fully remote arrangement receives an internet allowance of EUR 20 per month, paid automatically via payroll. You do not need to claim it. It appears as a separate line on your payslip.

### Equipment and security

Your Corvane laptop is your main work device. Request additional peripherals such as a docking station or headset through Assist. Personal devices must not be used to access customer data. See it-acceptable-use-policy for the rules that apply to all IT systems, at home or in the office.

## Special arrangements

### Fully remote roles

Fully remote work is possible in exceptional cases and needs director approval. Your manager raises the request with the director of your department, who decides together with People Operations. Fully remote employees are still expected to visit an office for team events when asked, with travel paid under hr-expense-travel-policy.

### Exceptions

Your manager may agree to a temporary change in your pattern, for example during building works or a short-term personal situation. Such changes should be confirmed in writing and reviewed at least every three months.

Exceptions on medical grounds or as a reasonable accommodation are handled by People Operations. Contact hr@example.com and we will discuss the options with you confidentially.

Managers can ask the team to be in the office on additional days, such as for planning sessions, customer visits or team events. Give at least one week's notice when you can.

New employees build their pattern with their manager in the same way as everyone else, but this limit is 2 days during probation, which lasts six months from your start date. Once your probation is confirmed in Orbit HR, the standard number of remote days applies from the following week.
