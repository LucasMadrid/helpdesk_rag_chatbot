# Vacation and Leave Policy

This policy explains how much vacation you have, how to book it, which other types of leave Corvane offers and which company holidays apply in 2026. It applies to all employees in Dublin, Lisbon and fully remote roles. All leave is requested and approved in Orbit HR at orbit.example.com.

## Vacation entitlement

How many vacation days do I have? Every full-time employee has 25 vacation days per calendar year. From your third year of service, you gain 1 extra day for every 2 years of service, up to a maximum of 30 days per year. Your current balance, including any carried-over days, is shown on the Time off page in Orbit HR. Company holidays are on top of your vacation days and are not deducted from your balance.

Part-time employees receive the same entitlement pro rata to their contracted hours, based on the 39-hour full-time week. In the year you join or leave, your entitlement is calculated pro rata from your start or end date.

### Entitlement by years of service

| Service year | Vacation days (full-time) | Extra days over base | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 25 | 0 | Pro rata from your start date |
| Year 2 | 25 | 0 | Full base entitlement |
| Years 3–4 | 26 | 1 | First tenure increase |
| Years 5–6 | 27 | 2 | Applied from 1 January of the year |
| Years 7–8 | 28 | 3 | Applied from 1 January of the year |
| Years 9–10 | 29 | 4 | Applied from 1 January of the year |
| Year 11 and later | 30 | 5 | Maximum entitlement |
| Part-time (any year) | Pro rata | Pro rata | Based on contracted hours out of 39 |

## Booking and carrying over vacation

Request vacation in Orbit HR and wait for your manager's approval before you make travel arrangements. For a block of 5 or more consecutive vacation days, give at least 10 working days' notice. Shorter absences can be requested at shorter notice, but your manager may decline if the team is understaffed. Managers are expected to respond within 3 working days.

You can carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the next year. Carried-over days have a grace period: they must be used by 31 March of the following year, after which any remaining carried-over days expire. Days above the 5-day limit expire on 31 December unless People Operations has approved an exception in writing, for example because a business-critical project prevented you from taking leave. Unused vacation is only paid out when you leave Corvane.

## Other types of leave

The table below summarises the other leave types. Paid allowances are per calendar year unless stated otherwise. Local law in Ireland or Portugal may give you more; where it does, the legal entitlement applies.

### Leave types at a glance

| Leave type | Paid allowance (days) | How to request |
|---|---|---|
| Vacation | 25 to 30, by service year | Orbit HR, approved by your manager |
| Sick leave | Up to 10 at full pay | Report to your manager on the first morning; certificate in Orbit HR from day 3 |
| Compensation day | 1 per holiday falling on a weekend | Added to your balance automatically by Orbit HR |
| Bereavement leave | Up to 5 per bereavement | Orbit HR, tell your manager as soon as you can |
| Marriage or civil partnership | 3 per occasion | Orbit HR, at least 10 working days ahead |
| Parental leave | See your contract | Contact People Operations at hr@example.com |
| Jury or court service | As required by the court | Orbit HR with a copy of the summons |
| Exam leave | 1 per exam, up to 5 | Orbit HR, for courses agreed with your manager |
| Volunteering day | 1 | Orbit HR, for a registered charity |
| Emergency family leave | Up to 3 | Tell your manager, then record in Orbit HR |

## Company holidays

Both offices observe the company holidays listed below. Lisbon also observes Portuguese national holidays, and Dublin also observes Irish bank holidays; these are shown in the holiday calendar in Orbit HR for your location. Fully remote employees follow the calendar of the office they are assigned to in Orbit HR.

When a company holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, you receive a compensation day: one extra day off that is added to your balance automatically and that you can book like a vacation day before 31 March of the following year. In 2026, 26 December falls on a Saturday, so every employee receives one compensation day.
