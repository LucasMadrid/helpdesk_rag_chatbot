# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Corvane. This guide walks you through your first day, your first week and your first month, so you know which accounts to set up, which equipment to expect and who can help you. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will go through it with you, but you can also use it as a checklist on your own.

Your probation period is 6 months. During that time you meet your manager regularly to review how things are going, and your probation is confirmed in Orbit HR at the end of it.

For questions about contracts, pay or Orbit HR, contact People Operations at hr@example.com or ext. 4470, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00. For IT problems, contact the IT Service Desk through Assist, at servicedesk@example.com or ext. 4357, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00 Irish time.

## Day 1

Your first day starts at 09:30 in the office you were hired for, or on a video call at 09:30 local time if you are fully remote. Your manager or your onboarding buddy meets you and takes you through the rest of the day.

### Accounts

#### Corvane ID

About two working days before your start date you receive an activation link for your Corvane ID at your personal email address. Corvane ID is the single sign-on account for almost every system you will use, including Outlook, Teams, Orbit HR and Assist. Open the link, choose your password and follow the steps on id.example.com. The link is valid for 72 hours; if it has expired, call the IT Service Desk on +353 1 555 0100 and ask for a new one.

#### Corvane Verify

When you first sign in you are asked to register the Corvane Verify app on your phone for multi-factor authentication. Install it from your phone's app store before your first day if you can. See it-password-mfa-policy for the current password and MFA rules.

#### Email and Teams

Your work email address and Teams account are ready on Day 1. Check that you can send an email, join a Teams call and see your team's channels.

### Equipment

Your laptop is shipped to your home address 3 working days before your start date, so it should be waiting for you on Day 1. Do not open the device before your first day unless your manager has asked you to. When you switch it on, sign in with your Corvane ID; DeviceDock then installs the standard software automatically, which can take up to an hour.

If the laptop has not arrived or arrives damaged, tell your manager and contact the IT Service Desk at servicedesk@example.com or ext. 4357. Office starters can collect a loan laptop from the Service Desk while the problem is fixed.

## Week 1

### Accounts

#### Orbit HR

Log in to Orbit HR at orbit.example.com and complete your profile: bank details, emergency contact, home address and tax information. Do this before the 10th of the month if you can, because that is the payroll cut-off; otherwise your first salary may be paid in the following month.

#### Lockbox and SecureLink

Set up your Lockbox password manager vault and store any work passwords that are not covered by Corvane ID. If you will work remotely, test the SecureLink VPN connection from home. See it-vpn-guide if you have any problems.

#### Team systems

Your manager requests access to team-specific systems through Assist. Raise a ticket yourself if anything is still missing at the end of the week.

### Equipment

Office starters are shown their desk area and given an access badge by Facilities. Request a headset, a docking station or an extra monitor through Assist if your team did not order them in advance. Software that is not installed automatically can be added from the DeviceDock Company Portal. See it-software-catalogue for what is available.

During Week 1 you are also introduced to your onboarding buddy, a colleague from a different team who supports you for 6 weeks. Your buddy is the person to ask the small questions you might not want to ask your manager.

## Month 1

### Accounts

#### Security training

Complete the mandatory security awareness training within 14 days of your start date. You find it in Orbit HR under Learning. If it is not completed in time, your manager is reminded automatically and some system access may be restricted until you finish it.

#### Review your access

By the end of your first month, check with your manager that you have all the access you need and nothing you do not. Report any unexpected access through Assist.

### Equipment

Check that your laptop shows as compliant in DeviceDock. If you see the message `Device not compliant`, see it-known-errors-faq. Home workers can claim the home-office allowance described in hr-remote-work-policy once they have set up their workspace.

At the end of Month 1 you have a first check-in with your manager about your goals for the rest of probation. Your buddy stays in touch until the end of week 6.
