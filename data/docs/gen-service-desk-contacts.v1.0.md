# Service Desk, Contacts and Support Levels

This page is the single source of truth for how to contact IT, Security and People Operations at Corvane, when each team is available, and how quickly you can expect a response. If another document gives a different number or time, this page is correct and the other document should be reported to the Service Desk for correction.

## Contacts

| Team | Email | Phone | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT Service Desk | servicedesk@example.com | ext. 4357 (+353 1 555 0100 from outside) | All IT problems and requests, including passwords, VPN, laptops and software |
| Security Operations | security@example.com | ext. 4357, then ask for Security | Phishing, suspicious MFA prompts, lost or stolen devices, data leaks |
| People Operations (HR) | hr@example.com | ext. 4470 | Leave, contracts, working abroad, policies; requests go through Orbit HR |
| Payroll & Benefits | hr@example.com | ext. 4470 | Payslips, pension, health insurance, allowances |

To report a phishing email, use the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. It sends the message to Security Operations and removes it from your inbox. You do not need to forward it as well.

## Where to raise a request

| System | Address | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Assist | assist.example.com | IT tickets and requests, tracking ticket status |
| Orbit HR | orbit.example.com | Leave, expenses, personal details, HR requests |
| Corvane ID | id.example.com | Password reset, MFA settings |
| DeviceDock Company Portal | App on your laptop | Installing approved software, checking device compliance |

Raising a ticket in Assist is the fastest route for anything that is not urgent, because it reaches the right team directly and keeps a record. Email to servicedesk@example.com creates an Assist ticket automatically but needs to be sorted by hand first.

Colleagues in Lisbon should note that Irish time is the same as Lisbon time all year, so the Service Desk hours are the same locally. The Service Desk is closed on company holidays; the P1 out-of-hours line stays open.

## Priorities and response times

The Service Desk sets the priority of each ticket based on impact and urgency. Response time is how long until a person starts working on the ticket. Resolution time is the target for restoring service or completing the request.

| Priority | Example | Response time | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Customer-facing platform down, security incident, many staff unable to work | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | One team blocked, a key system badly degraded | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 Medium | One person unable to use a system, with no workaround | 4 hours | 3 working days |
| P4 Low | Requests, questions, minor issues with a workaround | 1 working day | 10 working days |

A security incident is always P1, whatever its apparent size. This includes a phishing link you clicked, an MFA prompt you did not start, and a lost or stolen laptop or phone. If you think your ticket has the wrong priority, reply to it in Assist and explain the business impact.

## Escalation tiers

| Tier | Team | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Service Desk | First contact, password and MFA help, known errors, standard requests |
| Tier 2 | End-User Computing | Laptops, peripherals, software installs, device compliance |
| Tier 2 | Network & Connectivity | SecureLink VPN, office Wi-Fi, network access |
| Tier 3 | Infrastructure Engineering | Servers, cloud platforms, identity systems |
| Tier 3 | Security Operations | Security incidents, investigations, access exceptions |

You always start with Tier 1. The Service Desk passes tickets to Tier 2 or Tier 3 when needed, and you can follow progress in Assist. If a ticket has passed its resolution target without an update, reply in Assist asking for escalation, or call ext. 4357 and ask for the Service Desk team lead.

## Other teams

Questions for Finance, Facilities, Engineering, Sales or Customer Success do not go through the Service Desk. Use the team's channel in Teams or ask your manager for the right contact.
